# Implementation roadmap

The roadmap uses four decision-based phases. Dates are illustrative and depend on procurement,
legacy integration, staffing, and legal review.

| Phase | Indicative timing | Main outcome | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation | Months 0–2 | Ownership, baselines, service standards, architecture and control plan | Approved discovery pack and backlog |
| Pilot | Months 3–5 | Issue reporting and appointment booking for a limited resident group | User evidence, accessibility test, operational readiness |
| Integrate | Months 6–10 | Certificate and payment services connected to trusted systems | Reconciliation, recovery and security acceptance |
| Expand | Months 11–14 | Participation service and continuous improvement across the portfolio | Adoption review, funded ownership, benefits report |

## Delivery principles

- Redesign the process before digitising it.
- Keep assisted and physical channels available where residents need them.
- Release small service slices with observable outcomes.
- Fund operations, security, accessibility, and supplier management beyond launch.
- Stop or reshape a service when evidence does not support its expected benefit.

## Suggested KPIs

Track completion rate, median completion time, avoidable contact rate, accessibility defects,
failed transactions, payment reconciliation exceptions, service availability, incident recovery,
resident satisfaction, staff handling time, and assisted-channel demand. Segment results where
legally and ethically appropriate to detect exclusion.
